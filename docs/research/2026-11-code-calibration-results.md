Generated by `python scripts/calibrate_code.py report`. Human side: CodeMirage human files (GitHub code cleaned in May 2022). Frontier side: files written by today's Claude models from a summary of a human seed file. CodeMirage side: the same protocol run in 2025 by ten models (Claude 3.5 Haiku, GPT-4o-mini, o3-mini, DeepSeek V3 and R1, Gemini 2.0, Llama 3.3, Qwen2.5-Coder). A hit is slop when the code does not need what it matched, whoever wrote it (ADR 0003), so the shipped tier comes from judged precision, the share of sampled hits that were real (ADR 0010): S needs 90% of at least 8, M 70%. Rules with no judged hits keep their designed tier. Human and AI rates, and the likelihood ratio of frontier to human, say how often each pattern turns up, not whether a hit is real.
