# Fix

Remove the top reason buyers passed, or the one named, and show buyer by buyer what the change did and what it cost. `P` is the panel folder, `.buyer-panel/<slug>/`; `panel.py` is `python "${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/panel.py"`.

Read `RECORDS.md` before step 1, `INTERVIEW.md` before step 4, `SCORING.md` before step 5 and `READING.md` and `CRITIQUE.md` before step 8 (all in this folder). Bold steps without a number (**Base round**, **Checks**, **Critique**, **Sweep**) are in SKILL.md, steps the modes share.

1. **Base round.** The **Base round** (SKILL.md, steps the modes share). The screening rounds are `r<k+1>-a`, `-b`, `-c`, the full round `r<k+1>`. Done when the base summary exists.
2. **Reason.** The word after `fix` names a reason tag; without one, take the first of the base summary's `pass_reasons`. A named tag that is not among them: show the pass reasons with counts and ask with options, the top one first. The **reason-givers** are the base rows with decision PASS and that reason; list each with buyer id, choice and quote exactly as `summary.json` holds them. Fewer than three: say the screen will rest on too few buyers to rank the changes, and ask with options whether to screen anyway or stop. Done when the reason and its reason-givers are listed.
3. **Three changes.** Write three changes to the offer, each one a different way to answer the reason (what the product does, how it is sold or priced, how you switch to it), each tied to the quotes of the reason-givers it answers, by buyer id. A change stays a seller's offer on a neutral card (INTERVIEW.md, the cards): no answer to a buyer by name, no promise the product cannot keep. A change that moves the price says so. Show the three with their quotes and ask with options: **screen all three** (recommended), **replace one** (say which and how), **stop**. Done when the user chose to screen.
4. **Screen.** For each change, create `P/rounds/<label>/` (`r<k+1>-a` to `-c`) with a `round.json` that copies the base's and changes `label`, `offer` and, when the change moves it, `price`, and adds `base` (the base label), `fix` (the reason tag), `change` (the change in one line) and `screen: true`. Deal each with `panel.py deal --buyers P/buyers.jsonl --options <ids> --batch-size <n> --out P/rounds/<label>/cards.jsonl` and copy the seed. Interview only the reason-givers, in fresh contexts: run the `orchestrate` skill when installed, job `buyer-panel-<slug>-<k+1>-screen`, profile `light`, state dir `P/rounds/`, units = each screening round's reason-givers' cards in batches of `batch_size` in `cards.jsonl` order (a unit never holds two changes), unit brief = INTERVIEW.md's worker brief with that round's cards, each worker writing only `P/rounds/<label>/answers/batch-<n>.jsonl`. Without orchestrate, interview them one at a time as RUN.md step 9 says. Done when `panel.py validate` exits 0 on every answers file and every reason-giver has an answer in each screening round.
5. **Rank.** Judge each screening round apart (RUN.md step 10, one judge context per round), then `panel.py aggregate` each, its advisory **Checks** (`--advisory`: the reason-givers are a subset of the panel), and `panel.py diff P/rounds/<base> P/rounds/<label> --out P/rounds/<label>/diff.json`: the diff compares only the reason-givers, since nobody else saw the change. Rank the changes by PASS to BUY among them, then by the discounted change, then by the card price moved least. A tie at the top: ask with options which to run on the panel, each option naming its flips. Done when one change is chosen and every screening diff is kept.
6. **Full round.** Create `P/rounds/r<k+1>/` with the chosen change's `round.json` minus `screen`, plus `screened_in` (the screening label), and run RUN.md steps 8 to 11 on it: a new deal over the whole panel, every buyer in a fresh context that saw no screening round, its own judge, `aggregate --out summary.json`. Done when `aggregate` exits 0 on the round.
7. **Diff.** `panel.py diff P/rounds/<base> P/rounds/r<k+1> --out P/rounds/r<k+1>/diff.json`, then once more without `--json` for the Markdown. Done when it exits 0.
8. **Report.** The **Critique** on `r<k+1>`, then write `P/rounds/r<k+1>/report.md` per READING.md, with its Checks section from step 6 and three sections between the summary and the verdict: **The change** (the reason, the reason-givers' quotes, the three changes and for each its screening flips, discounted change from its `diff.json` and its advisory checks line when one printed); **What changed** (the diff's Markdown, unchanged); **What it cost** (the buyers who went BUY to PASS, each with the reason and quote from the diff's rows, and the card price and price range before and after). A net change of one or two buyers is noise at this size: write that, never "the fix worked". The verdict says whether to keep the change, and the first real test probes the reason on real people. Done when the report holds every number as the script printed it.
9. **Sweep.** The **Sweep** on `report.md`.
10. **History.** Append one line per round to `P/history.md`, the screening rounds first: date, label, `fix <reason>` and the base label, offer, price, raw and discounted BUY share (for a screening round, over the reason-givers), PASS to BUY and BUY to PASS, and for the full round the verdict.

**Completion.** Three screening rounds and the full round validate and aggregate with exit 0; every screening round holds only reason-givers and each has its `diff.json`; every screening round has an advisory `checks.json`; the full round reached every buyer in fresh contexts and its `checks.json`, `critique.md`, `diff.json` and report exist; the report opens with the simulated-panel line, shows the Checks section and the devil's advocate, says "Do not trust this round" in its verdict when a check of the full round failed, ties each change to buyers' quotes by id, shows the diff and the cost unchanged from the script, and ends with a verdict and one real test; the history has a line for each of the four rounds.
