# Ground

Import what real people did, compare it with what the panel predicted for the same offer at the same price, and store the ratio for the whole project so later rounds, of this panel or any later one, report a corrected figure. `P` below is the panel folder, `.buyer-panel/<slug>/`; `panel.py` is `python "${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/panel.py"`.

Read `RECORDS.md` (in this folder), sections "Real results" and "grounding.jsonl", before step 2.

1. **Panel.** With one panel in the ledger, use it; with several, show each one's last history line and ask with options which panel the results belong to. No panel: say that `ground` compares real results with a panel's prediction, and offer `run`. Done when `P` is known.
2. **Results.** Ask what was run, with options: **a smoke test** (visits and conversions), **interviews** (interviews held and how many ended with a commitment: a deposit, a signed letter of intent, a date to start), **a pre-sale** (people asked and people who paid). Then ask for the counts, the dates, where the numbers come from (analytics export, notes, payment records) and the price charged, unless the user's message already gives them. Numbers come from the user's records: never fill a count from the panel, from memory or from a guess. Done when every field of the real-results record has a value the user gave.
3. **Round.** The results are compared with the round that showed the same offer at the same price. Read `P/history.md` and each round's `round.json`; with one match, use it; with several, ask with options which one the real test used; with none, say so and offer to run a round with the offer and price tested (`run`, step 8 on), then ground against it. Done when one round label is chosen.
4. **Record.** Write `P/real/<label>.json` (`<label>` short and unique: `smoke-1`, `talks-1`, `presale-1`). Done when `panel.py validate P/real/<label>.json` exits 0.
5. **Compare.** `panel.py ground P/real/<label>.json`. It checks the price and currency against the round, recomputes the round's discounted BUY share, and writes the ratio of the real rate to it into `.buyer-panel/grounding.jsonl`, the project's ledger beside the panel folders, naming this panel; importing the same label again for this panel replaces its line. Exit 3 names the field at fault: a price that differs means another round, never an edited price. Done when it exits 0.
6. **Tell.** Print the script's line, then say in plain words how far the panel was off: a ratio of 0.35 means the panel's discounted share was about three times what people did; above 1, people acted more than the panel predicted. Name what it rests on: a smoke test with fewer than 100 visits or fewer than 10 interviews is thin, and the next grounding replaces it.
7. **History.** Append one line to `P/history.md`: date, `ground`, the label, the test, the round, the real counts, the ratio.

From now on every subcommand that prints a BUY share (`aggregate`, `diff`, `competitor`, `compare`, `revenue`, `quotes`, `objections` and `page`), on any panel of the project, prints a `Corrected` line under it: the discounted share times the latest grounding's ratio, in the units the real test measured (conversions per visit, commitments per interview, payments per person asked), with the grounding and the panel it came from. A round aggregated with other weights, or priced per another period than the round the real test used (per booking against per month), gets no corrected figure and says why. Reports carry the line as printed (READING.md).

**Completion.** `P/real/<label>.json` validates; `ground` exited 0 and `.buyer-panel/grounding.jsonl` holds the label's line for this panel; the user was told the ratio, which round it was compared with and how much it rests on; the history has the line; no count came from anywhere but the user's records.
